# ROMS Glossary

## Candidate
A person applying for a job or being managed through the recruitment pipeline.

## Workflow stage
The current recruiting milestone such as application, document verification, interview, or deployment.

## Branch
A regional or office-level unit of the recruitment operation.

## Passport inventory
The record of passport custody and movement.

## Commission
Payment due to the agency or office based on candidate outcome.

## Document upload
Any PDF, image, or file attached to a candidate profile.

## Snapshot
A recorded cloud backup containing the database dump and uploaded files for a certain time.

## Restore
The act of reloading a stored backup onto the active system.

## Audit log
The record of actions performed in the system.
