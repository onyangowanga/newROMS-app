# Recruiter User Manual

## Role summary

Recruiters are employer accounts. They submit their own job orders and review candidates assigned to those orders.

## Main responsibilities

- create job orders for their own employer account
- view only their own job orders in the signed-in workspace
- review assigned candidates, application status and recruitment progress
- preview or download those candidates' documents
- work with Branch Admins for escalations

## Login

1. Open the ROMS login page.
2. Enter your username and password.
3. If required, complete the password setup flow.

## Working with candidates

### Create a job order

1. Open My job orders and select Create job order.
2. Enter the job title, type, destination, headcount, contract period, expected salary range and qualifications.
3. Your own employer account is selected automatically and cannot be changed.
4. Submit the order. Super Admin or Branch Admin must approve it before it becomes public.

### Review assigned candidates

1. Open My candidates.
2. Review the candidate, associated job, application status and recruitment stage.
3. Select Documents to preview or download files.

### Review candidate applications

Only applications linked to a job owned by your account are visible. Candidates may have other applications, but those are not included in your list. Candidate records and document uploads are maintained by agency staff, not employer users.

## Access boundaries

Recruiters cannot see commission, paid totals, balances, financial statements or receipts, or export the agency's candidate workbook. They cannot add/change/delete candidates, alter their workflow, or upload/delete their documents. They cannot administer users or recruiters.

Existing job orders with no recruiter account link must first be assigned by an administrator. Employer names alone do not grant access. Open positions remain visible on the separate public applicant portal; the signed-in employer workspace is ownership-scoped.

## Best practice

- specify complete qualifications and a correct salary currency/range
- keep document records complete
- ask agency staff to correct unclear application or workflow statuses
- escalate missing or conflicting information to the Branch Admin or Super Admin
